## Product Type

---

# 2. Purpose

Bloomora is designed to simplify gifting by using Artificial Intelligence to recommend personalized gifts and connect customers with trusted local businesses.

The platform will support customers, business partners, delivery partners, and administrators.

---

# 3. Objectives

• Help customers choose gifts easily.

• Reduce decision fatigue.

• Build an AI-first gifting experience.

• Connect multiple partner stores.

• Support same-day delivery.

• Provide premium customer experience.

• Build a scalable marketplace.
