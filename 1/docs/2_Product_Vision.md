# Vision Statement

Bloomora exists to transform the way people choose and send gifts.

Instead of forcing customers to browse hundreds of products, Bloomora understands the person they are gifting and recommends the perfect gift using Artificial Intelligence.

We believe gifting should feel personal, effortless, and memorable.

---

# Why Bloomora Exists

Buying gifts should be exciting.

Instead, most people experience confusion.

Common questions include:

• What should I gift?

• Will they like it?

• Is my budget enough?

• Which bouquet looks better?

• Which shop should I choose?

• Can I customize it?

Bloomora removes these questions.

Instead of searching for products, customers simply describe the person they want to surprise.

Bloomora does the rest.
