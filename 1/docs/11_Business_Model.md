# Purpose

This document explains how Bloomora generates revenue, creates value for customers and partners, and scales into a sustainable AI-powered gifting marketplace.

---

# Business Model

Bloomora is an AI-powered multi-vendor gifting marketplace.

Bloomora does not manufacture products.

Bloomora connects customers with trusted local partner businesses using Artificial Intelligence.

Bloomora earns revenue by enabling better gifting experiences.
