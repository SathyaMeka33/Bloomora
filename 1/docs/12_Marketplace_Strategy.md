# Purpose

This document defines how Bloomora operates as an AI-powered multi-vendor gifting marketplace.

It explains partner onboarding, order lifecycle, commission structure, delivery workflow, quality standards, and future expansion.

---

# Marketplace Vision

Bloomora is not a flower shop.

Bloomora is a technology platform connecting customers with trusted gifting partners.

Customers should remember Bloomora, not individual stores.

Partners focus on creating beautiful gifts.

Bloomora manages technology, customer experience, AI recommendations, and marketplace operations.

---

# Marketplace Philosophy

One Platform.

Many Trusted Partners.

One Premium Experience.

Customers should receive the same quality regardless of which partner fulfills the order.
