# Bloomora Development Roadmap

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Last Updated: July 2026

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the complete execution plan for building Bloomora.

The objective is to launch a stable, scalable, AI-powered gifting platform that can expand from Rajahmundry to India and eventually international markets.
