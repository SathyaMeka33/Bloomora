# Purpose

This document defines Bloomora's complete marketing strategy from launch in Rajahmundry to international expansion.

Marketing is not about selling products.

Marketing is about building trust, emotions, and memorable experiences.

---

# Marketing Vision

Bloomora should become the first brand people remember whenever they need a meaningful gift.

Customers should think

"I need a gift."

↓

"Let's check Bloomora."

without comparing multiple websites.

---

# Marketing Philosophy

We don't sell bouquets.

We create smiles.

We don't sell gifts.

We create memories.

We don't advertise products.

We tell stories.
