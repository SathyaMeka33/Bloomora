# Bloomora AI Strategy

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

Last Updated: July 2026

# Purpose

This document defines how Artificial Intelligence powers every major experience inside Bloomora.

AI is not an additional feature.

AI is the foundation of Bloomora.

Every customer should feel guided, understood, and confident while choosing gifts.

---

# AI Vision

Bloomora will become the world's smartest gifting assistant.

Instead of asking customers to search products, Bloomora understands emotions, relationships, occasions, budgets, and preferences before recommending gifts.

Our goal is to remove confusion from gifting.

---

# AI Mission

Help every customer choose the perfect gift with confidence.

Reduce decision fatigue.

Increase personalization.

Support partner businesses.

Create memorable gifting experiences.

---

# Core AI Philosophy

AI should never replace human emotions.

AI should understand emotions.

AI should simplify decisions.

AI should explain recommendations.

AI should continuously improve.
